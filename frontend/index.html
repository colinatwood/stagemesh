What it can do

One executable for the production workflow

Show control

Coordinate transport, timing, stage state, player views, and graceful degradation when systems change.

Audio and MIDI

Connect native audio devices and MIDI controllers with explicit ownership, lifecycle, routing, and loss handling.

Stage design

Build reusable stage templates with performers, microphones, monitors, speakers, lights, markers, and venue-aware layouts.

Media and DAW work

Arrange clips non-destructively, add markers and automation, prepare takes, and plan production exports.

Lighting and compatibility

Prepare Art-Net and sACN targets, inspect venue capabilities, and propose controlled adaptations before output.

Operator safety

Keep software readiness, physical hardware qualification, and live output authority separate and explicit.

StageMesh is open source. The desktop application is under active development; published builds will include platform-specific qualification notes.
